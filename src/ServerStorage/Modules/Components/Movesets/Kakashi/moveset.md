# Zakashi Ataque
**Role:** Affliction
**Element:** Electric

> Passive:
* When hitting any Electric attack, obtain a 'Lightning' charge, up to 6, once 6 are obtained, hold 'Basic Attack' to enter **Lightning Mode**, user's arms get a slight electric aura and his clothes start shining slightly blue on the arms side.
* **Paralyze**: Non-boss enemy actions can all be interrupted, and resistance to all elemental attacks is reduced.

> Basic Attacks
* Simple 6 hit combo, using two quick punches, a sweep into a kunai and then two kicks mid air. 
* When in **Lightning Mode**, the first two punches and the last kick will be of Electric affliction, and they'll reduce the enemies resistance to Daze.

> Dodge Counter
* By default, Zakashi will use 'Raiton: Raiju Tsuiga' and attack the enemy infront of him using a lightning dog, applying **Paralyze** on the enemies
* When in **Lightning Mode**, Zakashi will kick the enemies infront of him upwards, using 'Shishi Rendan', but after the final kick, he will use 'Raikiri' and strike the ground, causing an electric explosion.

> Assist
* By default, Zakashi will sub in and use 'Raikiri: Issen', dashing foward and hitting the first target in front of him, except the hit will produce an electric discharge.
* When in **Lightning Mode**, Zakashi will switch to 'Raiton: Raiju Tsuiga', first using Shadow Clone jutsu to split himself, and then casting the jutsu, spawning two lightning dogs that go forward and inflict heavy Electric Affliction buildup, applying **Paralyze** on hit,  enemies hit are stunned, and their resistance to electric affliction is reduced (faster buildup%)

> Special
* Zakashi will use 'Raikiri', casting a lightning cutter and running forward, hitting only one enemy.

> EX Special
* Zakashi will use 'Raikiri: Sōsenkō', casting a double raikiri, one in each hand, any enemy hit by the first one will be grabbed along, and hit with the second raikiri. Swiftly casting again this attack after having hit 'Raikiri: Sosenko', will have kakashi jump back and use 'Kagebunshin Jutsu', spawning a clone of him, and using 'Raiden' to create a lightning blade between him and his clone, any enemy hit by the blade will be paralyzed for half a second.
* When in **Lightning Mode**, 'Raiden' will be replaced with 'Raikiri: Denko Rensen', instead of using 'Kagebunshin Jutsu', Zakashi will hit enemies infront of him many times while moving left and right in a Zig-Zag pattern. 

> Chain Attack
* Kakashi subs in and uses 'Raikiri: Denko Rensen'.

> Ultimate
* Zakashi will use 'Raikiri: Sōraishin', he will dash forward and upon first enemy contact, throw every enemy in a small area into the air, consecutively hitting them in zig-zag mid air, after which he will release them and strike all of the hit enemies down with a final Raikiri.
